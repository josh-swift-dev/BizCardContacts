Privacy Policy

BizCard Contacts · Effective September 17, 2026

Privacy Summary: BizCard Contacts is designed to work locally on your device. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking.

Information You Add to the App

BizCard Contacts allows you to scan or import business cards and organize the information contained on them. Depending on the business card and any edits you make, this information may include names, company names, job titles, phone numbers, email addresses, websites, postal addresses, notes, tags, and images of business cards.

On-Device Processing and Storage

Business card recognition and text extraction are designed to be performed on your device using Apple platform technologies.

BizCard Contacts stores its application data locally on your device. BizCard Contacts does not operate an account system or a developer-operated cloud database for your scanned business cards.

Camera Access

BizCard Contacts requests access to your device's camera when you choose to scan a business card. Camera access is used only to provide scanning functionality that you initiate.

Photo Library Access

If you choose to import a business card image from your photo library, BizCard Contacts uses the photo or photos you select for that action.

Contacts Access

If you choose to add scanned information to Apple Contacts, BizCard Contacts may request Contacts permission or display Apple's contact interface as required by the feature.

BizCard Contacts does not upload your address book to a developer-operated server.

Exports, Sharing, and Backups

BizCard Contacts can create files such as vCards, CSV exports, and backup files when you request them.

When you export or share information, you choose the destination. This may include Apple Files, Mail, Messages, cloud storage, or another application installed on your device.

Information you choose to export or share is subject to the privacy practices of the destination or service you select.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

BizCard Contacts does not include third-party advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking.

BizCard Contacts does not sell personal information.

Network Access

The core business card scanning, OCR, organization, and local storage features do not require a BizCard Contacts account or developer-operated online service.

When you intentionally open a website, email address, map location, telephone action, or sharing destination, iOS or another selected application or service may use an internet connection to complete that action.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored by BizCard Contacts remains under your control on your device until you delete it, remove the app and its local data, restore or replace device data, or otherwise manage it using Apple-provided device features.

BizCard Contacts also provides controls for deleting saved records.

Files or information that you previously exported or shared must be managed separately at their destination.

Children's Privacy

BizCard Contacts is a general business productivity application and is not directed toward children.

Security

BizCard Contacts relies on iOS security features and application sandboxing for locally stored application data. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect access to their devices and maintain appropriate backups of important information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when BizCard Contacts features or privacy practices change. The effective date displayed at the top of this page identifies the current version.
