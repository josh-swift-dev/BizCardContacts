Business Card Scanner · Scan · Organize · Connect

Welcome to BizCard Contacts.

BizCard Contacts turns physical business cards into an organized, searchable business contact directory. Scan a card or import an existing photo, review the detected information, and save it to your directory.

Quick Start

1. Scan or import a business card
Open BizCard Contacts and choose the option to scan a business card or import an existing card image from Photos.
2. Review the detected information
BizCard Contacts analyzes the card and attempts to identify information such as the person's name, company, job title, phone number, email, website, and address.
3. Correct anything that needs attention
Review the detected information and edit any field that was not recognized correctly.
4. Save the card
Save the reviewed card to add it to your BizCard Contacts directory.
5. Find it later
Use search, favorites, tags, and notes to quickly find and organize your saved business contacts.

Scanning a Business Card

To scan a physical business card:

  1. Open BizCard Contacts.
  2. Choose the option to scan a business card.
  3. Allow camera access if prompted.
  4. Place the business card on a flat surface.
  5. Position the entire card within the camera view.
  6. Allow the scanner to detect and capture the card.
  7. Continue scanning additional cards if desired.
  8. Finish the scanning session when you are done.

After scanning, BizCard Contacts processes the captured card and prepares the recognized information for review.

Tips for Better Scans

  • Use bright, even lighting.
  • Avoid glare from glossy cards.
  • Place the card on a contrasting background.
  • Keep the entire card visible.
  • Keep the card flat whenever possible.
  • Avoid heavy shadows across the card.
  • Make sure small text is in focus.

Scanning Multiple Business Cards

BizCard Contacts supports scanning multiple cards during a scanning session.

  1. Scan the first business card.
  2. Continue to the next card when ready.
  3. Repeat for the remaining cards.
  4. Finish the scanning session.
  5. Review each captured card and its recognized information.
  6. Save the cards you want to keep.

Review each card carefully before saving it to your directory.

Importing a Business Card from Photos

If you already have a picture of a business card:

  1. Open the card capture options.
  2. Choose the option to import from Photos.
  3. Select the business card image.
  4. Allow BizCard Contacts to analyze the image.
  5. Review the detected information.
  6. Make any necessary corrections.
  7. Save the card.

BizCard Contacts only uses photos that you choose through the provided photo-selection interface.

Reviewing a Scanned Card

OCR, or Optical Character Recognition, allows BizCard Contacts to read printed information from a business card.

Depending on the card, the app may recognize information including:

  • First and last name
  • Company or organization
  • Job title
  • Phone numbers
  • Email addresses
  • Websites
  • Street addresses

Always review the results before saving. Business cards use many different fonts, layouts, logos, colors, and designs, so automated recognition may occasionally make a mistake.

Editing Contact Information

Before saving a card, review the available fields and correct anything that is incomplete or inaccurate.

You can also add information that may not have appeared on the original business card.

Your Business Card Directory

Saved cards are stored in your BizCard Contacts directory.

Your directory provides a central place to browse and locate the business contacts you have collected.

Searching for a Contact

Use the search field to quickly locate saved business contacts.

Search can help you find a contact without manually scrolling through your entire collection.

Favorites

Use Favorites for contacts you want to access frequently.

Mark a saved business contact as a favorite and it will also appear in the Favorites area of BizCard Contacts.

You can remove a contact from Favorites without deleting the saved contact itself.

Tags and Notes

Tags and notes can help you remember why a contact is important or where you met them.

Examples include:

  • Customer
  • Vendor
  • Conference
  • Sales Lead
  • Partner
  • Networking Event

You can also use notes to record additional context about a business contact.

Viewing the Original Business Card

When a business card image is available, BizCard Contacts keeps the saved card image with the contact record.

This lets you refer back to the original card if you need to verify information or simply want to see the physical card again.

Opening a Saved Contact

Select a saved contact from your directory to view its details.

Depending on the information stored for the contact, available actions may include:

  • Calling the contact
  • Sending an email
  • Opening the contact's website
  • Viewing an address in Maps
  • Adding the information to Apple Contacts
  • Sharing contact information

Adding a Person to Apple Contacts

BizCard Contacts can help transfer saved business information into Apple Contacts.

  1. Open the saved business contact.
  2. Select the available Contacts action.
  3. Review the information presented by iOS.
  4. Make any additional changes if needed.
  5. Complete the save using Apple's Contacts interface.

iOS may request Contacts permission when this feature is used.

Calling a Contact

If a saved business contact contains a telephone number, select the phone action to hand the number off to iOS.

Standard carrier and telephone service conditions may apply.

Emailing a Contact

If an email address is available, select the email action to begin an email using an available mail application on your device.

Opening a Website

If the business card contains a website, select the website action to open it using the appropriate iOS web browser or service.

Opening an Address in Maps

If a postal address is stored with the contact, use the map action to open the address using Apple Maps.

Sharing Contact Information

BizCard Contacts can create a vCard for supported contact-sharing actions.

When the iOS share sheet appears, choose where you want to send or save the contact.

Once information is shared outside BizCard Contacts, it is managed by the destination you selected.

Exporting Your Directory

BizCard Contacts provides a CSV export option for your business contact directory.

To export your directory:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Locate the export options.
  3. Select the CSV export option.
  4. Confirm the export if prompted.
  5. Choose where you want to save or share the file.

CSV files can be useful for viewing or working with contact information in compatible spreadsheet and data applications.

Creating a Complete Backup

BizCard Contacts also provides a complete backup option.

The backup is designed to preserve your saved BizCard Contacts directory and associated saved business card images.

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Select the complete backup option.
  3. Confirm the backup.
  4. Wait while BizCard Contacts prepares the backup.
  5. Select a location in Files or another available destination.

Keep important backups in a safe location that you control.

Deleting a Business Contact

Use the available delete control for a saved record when you no longer want to keep it.

Deleting a contact from BizCard Contacts does not automatically delete copies that you previously exported, shared, or separately saved to Apple Contacts.

Deleting All BizCard Contacts Data

If you use the application's option to delete all saved information, review the confirmation carefully before continuing.

Deletion may not be reversible unless you have separately preserved the information in an appropriate backup.

Permissions Used by BizCard Contacts

Camera

Camera access is used when you choose to scan a physical business card.

Photos

Photo access is used when you choose an existing business card image from your photo library.

Contacts

Contacts access may be used when you choose to create or work with an Apple Contacts entry.

If you previously denied a permission, you can review the permissions available to BizCard Contacts in the iPhone Settings app.

Troubleshooting

The Business Card Scanner Doesn't Open

Apple's document camera requires supported camera hardware. Test business card scanning on a physical iPhone rather than relying solely on the iOS Simulator.

Also verify that camera access has been allowed for BizCard Contacts.

The Scanner Has Trouble Detecting a Card

  • Move the card to a surface with better contrast.
  • Improve the lighting.
  • Reduce glare.
  • Keep all four edges of the card visible.
  • Hold the iPhone steady.

The Recognized Information Is Incorrect

OCR results can vary based on card design and image quality.

Try scanning the card again with improved lighting and focus. You can also manually correct the information during review.

A Field Wasn't Detected

If BizCard Contacts does not recognize a particular field, enter the information manually before saving the card.

I Can't Add the Contact to Apple Contacts

Verify that the required Contacts permission is enabled for BizCard Contacts. You can review app permissions in the iPhone Settings app.

I Can't Import a Photo

Verify that the image is available in your Photos library and that you selected it through the BizCard Contacts photo picker.

My Export Is Missing

When exporting or creating a backup, iOS allows you to select the destination. Check the folder or application you selected during the export process.
